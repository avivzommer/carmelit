# Gameplay clearance

- Never route the mechanic through walls, closed doors, train bodies, or scenery.
- Every route must have a continuous walking surface and room for the mechanic's body and head.
- Check pedestrian routes against actual geometry at all train stops and throughout train movement. Graph connectivity alone does not prove a route is playable.
- Trains must board through aligned open side doors. Wait for doors and gates to clear before walking across them.
- Keep scenery behind gameplay. If valid architecture hides the mechanic, use a visual cutaway without disabling its collision.
